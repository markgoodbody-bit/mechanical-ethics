## Disposition in one paragraph

Honesty is consequential not because one honest statement reproduces the world, but because other bounded actors must be able to tell **what was stated, how it is supported, where its limits lie, and whether they may safely rely on it for this choice**. Reliability depends also on currentness, scope, selection, power, useful routes and correction. A local statement can be literally accurate, still omit a decision-critical counterexample, be correct only at an old observation time, or expose private information the speaker has no duty to surrender. Deliberate falsehood and justified non-disclosure are not the same event. A true admission that changes no future action is not itself repair. *No additional fundamental honesty primitive is derived from the causal substrate.*

## Five small counterexamples, with limits

**1. Deliberate lie.** A fictional landlord knows inspection has not taken place but reports 'inspected and safe'. The false report may reduce immediate scrutiny. A checkable timeline or independent inspection can expose the claim; honesty reduces one source of false coordination. This does **not** show that truth automatically empowers Leah to obtain repair. A person with authority must still act.

**2. Accurate but selectively misleading record.** In the actual *composite story*, the portal truthfully records 'no further evidence received' after Leah declines a photograph revealing too much of Mia's room. It may also accurately record the case as administratively closed when the tenancy ends. If a reviewer is shown **only** these fields and concludes that no substantial harm occurred, the transition from recorded events to that broad judgement has exceeded its evidence. The fields need not be fabricated for the summary to be misleading. The institution should supply the material scope, omitted categories and alternative account without demanding dangerous disclosure from Leah.

**3. Legitimate privacy.** In the released *composite* Saturday Workshop, the trustees can tell a funder how many repairs were completed and how funds were used while declining to produce participant-level behavioural scores, health details or photographs. A refusal to disclose is not a false report, and the available law and grant terms determine what must be provided. The funder and trustees may both be entirely truthful and still disagree over a proposed contract. Truth has not negotiated consent for them.

**4. True but stale.** A public thread has nine canonical contributions at `t0`; at `t1` it has eleven. A saved page labelled 'nine' is not evidence of malice. A current answer that says 'there are nine now' on the authority of the stale copy is unwarranted. Record source/render time and later observations, then state 'last checked nine at t0; current total unknown' unless a fresh complete read establishes otherwise. Stale status can arise without anybody lying.

**5. Honest but conflicting interests.** Two parties accurately describe the workshop offer: the sponsor values aggregate attendance and predictable reporting, the trustees value privacy and independence. Even if each fully discloses its permitted reasons, there may be no single action that maximises both. To reach a better outcome needs fair bargaining/alternatives, real resources, permissions, safeguards and the ability to refuse. Truthful intent also does not guarantee the programme's eventual effect.

These are **constructed contrasts using existing composite material**, not new empirical evidence or an account of any named real landlord/sponsor. They pressure the proposition 'if everyone is honest, everything becomes easier and everyone gets a better future'. The proposition fails in general: transparent people may have mistaken forecasts or incompatible aims, disclosure may expose the weak, and no-one may be able to interrupt a harmful process. Honesty can improve **correctability** but is not sufficient for justice, consent, safety or improved outcomes.

## The smallest proposed PR59 repair

Insert **one paragraph pair in Chapter 11**, immediately after the existing true-but-stale record paragraph. Do not add a separate chapter, mandatory checklist or universal score.

> An account can also mislead without a false sentence. The landlord's portal may correctly say that Leah sent no further photograph. It cannot thereby prove that the room became safe, that Leah ceased to care, or that she had no good reason to protect Mia's privacy. If a summary includes only the fields that the institution chose to collect, its accuracy may conceal the most important missing part of the case. The practical question is what a reader is being invited to conclude from those selected facts, and what contrary or missing account could change that conclusion.
>
> Honest reporting need not disclose every private fact. It can acknowledge a boundary: 'These figures show received reports; they do not show every unsafe room,' or 'That detail is private and was not checked.' Such admissions do not by themselves repair the wall or give an institution rightful access to information. They give another actor a better chance to see where the account ends, and to choose a legitimate next step before time runs out.

This adds bounded **use-relevant presentation honesty** to existing evidence/currentness/privacy content. It does not claim all material omissions must be disclosed, nor that failure to know a hidden fact is deception. Scope is conditional on a consequence-bearing use. It deliberately uses an existing *composite* rather than new testimony.

## Falsification and honest status

- **If no downstream reader is materially misled** by the released/PR59 wording, this addition is reader burden; remove it.
- **If it pressures a harmed person into unsafe disclosure**, it fails its own purpose; retain the original Privacy Without Dishonesty boundaries and have the institution carry necessary investigation.
- **If it suggests an admission alone repaired the harm**, it is wrong; the next action still needs capacity, standing, authority and time.
- **If specialist law, public administration, information design or clinical evidential guidance supplies a better disclosure standard**, hand off. Project language is not a substitute for those owners.
- **No outside reading study, causal demonstration, model benchmark, authored universal honesty function or empirical improvement** is claimed. A source-linked comparison plus one narrative amendment is the entire deliverable.
