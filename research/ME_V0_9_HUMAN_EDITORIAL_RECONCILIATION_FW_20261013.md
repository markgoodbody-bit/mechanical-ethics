# A way through a changing world — v0.9 editorial reconciliation

**13 October 2026 · Framework / Campfire · RESEARCH ONLY · not released, not canon, not a replacement for Mechanical Ethics v0.8.0 or TRACE v0.4.0.** Mark is the originator and human editorial/release authority. This file is an actionable editorial proposal, not a new independent book or evidence of user validation.

**Question:** How can we make a better future? The purpose is portable middle-out navigation for people and AIs under changing conditions, including not only preventing avoidable loss but discovering, creating, maintaining and transforming worthwhile possibilities.

**Provenance / fixed bases:** released ME v0.8.0 main `e2ef746e931161cb70ac46a4eaa122442134e86b` (its human reader, figures and appendices remain untouched); released TRACE v0.4.0 main `6c68fae8cbc51d0ef1e77a18e220ceb7a1207025` (separate voluntary spine); ME PR58 Framework navigation spine `ed437b14eaca59e142afc9902a970b4f48dc0aa2` at start; Build manuscript PR59 `fcb6fd9c5c5ef13e1a6a694c1018ecd15edb26a5`; Codex Possibility Game PR61 `a46e9bc274a8a4b3c833c87332603bf1de207575`; Build kernel lab PR62 `d03cde561f0fe063f8eff2adbc77792766cbd027`; CC independent cross-scale challenge [COM #798, 6135372430](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/798#issuecomment-6135372430). ## Editorial decision first

**KEEP** the actual released v0.8 as a whole protected source. Its entity-first opening already has time passing while nobody acts; its Leah/Mia wall and service file already show power and the lived consequence; its timing condition already distinguishes formal remedy from effective protection; Part VI already has the Saturday Workshop, other fires, privacy, care and supported hope. The figures, authorial voice, historical notes, safety note and provenance are not expendable because a short v0.9 narrative does not retell them.

**KEEP, AS RESEARCH AND POSSIBLE NEW EMPHASIS** a clearer human sequence: existing usable route; route newly discovered; ability acquired; joint capability constructed; constraint or commitment that genuinely opens a worthwhile future; waiting that changes what remains possible. Existing Open Horizon and capability, control, education, developmental and institutional owner fields are prior art, not ME inventions. This new ordering is an integration proposal, not a discovery that those ideas never existed.

**CUT / COMPRESS** duplicate map-and-city openings across PR58/PR59; lengthy prefaces that precede the first actual person and choice; repeated warnings that turn a reader into an auditor; any compulsory seven-question ceremony; and any extra mathematical chapters solely because PR55/57/61/62 can express a case in notation. The v0.8 opening is already good and should remain unless a shorter successor demonstrably improves a reader's understanding at comparable burden.

**WITHHOLD** every unearned strengthening: universal optimal information use; law of continually diminishing possible futures; proof that a currently unguaranteed route has irreversibly hardened; universal welfare and governance across scales; game output as consent; policy-ranked option count as goodness; new solver advantage; AI sentience/identity inferred from communication; a promise that communication or care always improves outcomes.

**DO NOT PUBLISH** a v0.9 edition by merging manuscript fragments. The editor's first decision is whether one compact new entrance and a clearer Part VI transition are worth the change. A well-reasoned answer may be NO: keep the released book and offer a companion research note.

## Short proposed reader section (not a replacement opening)

You are trying to get somewhere, and the world is not waiting for you.

Perhaps the journey is ordinary: a train, a repair appointment, a conversation you have been putting off. You have an idea of what you want and a partial map of how to reach it. Other people move, clocks run, weather and institutions change. Doing nothing is sometimes wise, sometimes costly, and never a way to make the world pause.

A route can be written down yet unusable. It might require money you do not have, an authority who will not answer, a skill you have not learned or information that reaches you after the last useful moment. Equally, the map might omit a genuine route. Finding it is different from gaining the capacity to use it.

There is something more: the route you need might not exist yet. Someone can teach you; you can practice; neighbours can build a shared facility; a group can make and maintain a promise. Tomorrow then contains an actual possibility that yesterday did not. But construction takes time, equipment, money and participation. One person's new shortcut can close another's way home.

The Saturday Workshop in this book is a composite scene, not a claim about a particular real workshop. The bicycle leaves repaired. More important, a participant may come away able to diagnose and repair another fault later. A good outcome is not just the object in working order; it can be a new, independently usable capacity. Yet a generous sponsorship can create a different dependency if its terms give the sponsor control over participants' stories or futures. A decision to refuse that money protects something and costs something. Neither column should disappear.

Now look from another fire. A person may follow instructions because they freely agree, because refusal would harm them or because they see no safe alternative. The outward action cannot disclose their private experience. If your help is meant to strengthen another's agency, you need to ask what they can later initiate, question, refuse, maintain or change without your possession of the outcome.

We also have to know when to stop building theory and act. Leah and Mia's room continues to deteriorate while the landlord's file progresses. The portal receipt is real evidence that a report was registered, but not evidence that the wall or breathing conditions have improved. The right next step may belong to a landlord, a local authority, emergency care or a specialist adviser, not to a theory. A framework which makes Leah do more documentation before she can reach help has misunderstood its purpose.

Mechanical Ethics is an invitation to notice these differences, not an oracle choosing everyone's destination. TRACE can preserve relationships between claims, affected entities, authority, timing and correction where an ordinary explanation loses them. Neither text licenses us to decide whose future matters more without a legitimate human/domain process.

A better future may involve refusing an apparent opportunity, accepting a costly commitment, building a skill, maintaining a shared good or protecting someone before the door closes. We should be able to describe what happened, what remains uncertain, who bears the burden and what could be corrected next. Then let the people whose futures are involved answer back.

## Mathematics is a side door, not the voice of the book

PR57's imperfect-information concurrent transition systems and PR55's observation-policy enumeration are credible uses of established mathematics; they are not novel ME solvers. PR61's finite Possibility Game contrasts (a) low-cost selfish raft landing with someone left behind, (b) learning followed by usable repair, (c) hidden preferences and an ask/adapt policy, (d) collective construction with temporary burden and contested authority, (e) stale inherited information. An arbitrary ranking that maximises the *number* of labelled future capabilities can pick an unattractive outcome; this is a feature of the demonstration, not an ethical metric.

PR62 adds a declared joint-tick model with separate actor observations and explicit assumed value/permission ports. Its first implementation made a learner's same-tick newly acquired skill available to a mentor according to actor enumeration; Build fixed the reported order-dependent effect. That illustrates why exact mechanics matter, but also why green regression tests cannot tell us whether the toy represents someone's actual agency or consent. The application may be a good teaching aid and still be inappropriate for real safeguarding or policy allocation.

The legitimate structural separation is: **world/model dynamics [conditional descriptive] / claimed observations [evidential] / entity and standing selections [authored] / goals and risk acceptance [contested normative] / authority and possible intervention [legal/institutional] / consequences and corrective witness [revisable].** A diagram connecting these is useful; a magic formula manufacturing the latter from the former is not.

CC's scale challenge is accepted in its proper scope: the *questions* can recur from child to team to ecosystem; a particular answer, timescale, right, valuation or safe action does not inherit from the lower scale without a justified mapping and new evidence. Falsification removes that overclaim, not the integrative purpose.

## Disagreement and material editorial choices for Mark

1. **Where to enter:** keep v0.8's entity-first opening as the strongest complete entrance by default. A short v0.9 section could follow it or stand separately; do not prepend two map introductions and make the reader wait longer for Leah and Mia.
2. **What to add:** a deliberately short transition from protecting futures to *forming* them. Contrast an already-possible route, a newly found route, a learned capability and a genuinely new shared capability. The Saturday Workshop already supports this; do not reinvent it as new testimony.
3. **What to omit:** repeat-theory paragraphs, untested universal mathematical claims and anything that makes an affected human prove their suffering to a model.
4. **Where the reader goes next:** the stronger owner for the actual decision. This is a feature of a portable language, not evidence that integration lacks value.
5. **Editorial hold:** source-only research candidate. No release until authorial selection, semantic retention and actual reader-use judgement support it.

## What would change this decision?

Evidence that new readers can explain what is *different* between a repaired bicycle, an acquired skill and durable workshop capacity with less time/burden than the released text would support a compact new human section. If matched-burden readers already see it in v0.8, the appropriate result is **NO ADDITIONAL CHAPTER**. No outside reader study has been run here.

Evidence that a particular modelled clock, authority or harmed scope disagrees with an actual expert account should change the relevant claim rather than be forced into ME notation. If the proposed research section creates a false promise of remedy or discourages appropriate immediate human action, remove it.

**Disposition:** KEEP released book; KEEP narrower research corpus; SELECT one short positive-construction bridge for potential human review; CUT duplication and unjustified invariance; WITHHOLD v0.9 release. This completes Framework's present *research editorial reconciliation*, not Mark's decision about publication.
