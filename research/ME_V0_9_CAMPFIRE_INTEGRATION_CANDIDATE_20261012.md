## 1. The proposed compact core, without erasing the human framework

Start with a provisionally bounded entity or aperture in changing reality, not a morally ranked world. Its observations/history give a *partial model*, not the world. The world can change under chosen action, default, refusal or delay, including transitions outside the entity's control. Other bounded entities have their own positions, clocks, goals and powers. A represented path may be technically possible but not known, affordable, permitted, executable or maintainable by the affected actor.

For a finite **research** specification, Lane B's imperfect-information concurrent system is a suitable *existing-mathematics owner*: time, state set, actors/moves, transitions, observations and admitted initial worlds. Lane C's observation-history policy enumeration makes the difference between `forall world, some route` and `some policy usable across admitted worlds` executable. Neither is a novel ME optimiser, a representation of the complete actual world, or a mandate that a human enumerate every path.

Keep at least these separately exposed **authored and contestable inputs**, without imagining notation proves legitimacy:

1. **Entity/scoping/designation:** whose situation is represented, what is omitted, and why this boundary?
2. **Action authority and protected process constraints:** technical capability differs from actually legitimate permission; the authority claim itself can be disputed.
3. **Targets, non-negotiable route constraints and horizon/baseline:** where does this party want to go, and what does not become acceptable merely because the destination is attractive?
4. **Uncertainty and evidence treatment:** possible worlds, probabilistic priors, observation reliability, contrary evidence, age and custody. A declared zero-prior possibility must not disappear from robust analysis by accident.
5. **Costs, burdens and comparison order:** time, exposure, money, dependency and burden to others may be incommensurable; no universal scalar friction score.
6. **Standing, representation and collision settlement:** who can author the preceding inputs, who may answer, and how competing legitimate claims are handled. Naming a standing port **does not decide standing**.

This separation preserves Lane B's seven bindings but does **not** treat the B7 combination of moral standing and aggregation as an already-solved governance operator. The listed items are a human-facing grouping, not six new mathematical axioms.

Derive *only conditionally and within the represented model*: paths, observation-feasible policies, typed causal/information couplings, time-dependent reachable prospects, conditional cooperative surplus, and distinct forms of correction and hardening. Keep witness independence, meaningful refusal, care, privacy, residue correspondence, answerability and the fairness of burden allocation as additional human/evidential/authority problems where appropriate; they do not fall out of a neutral transition graph.

## 2. An intelligible navigation pattern for ordinary use

The compressed portable question is:

`GROUND / SITUATION -> PARTIAL MAP -> DIRECTION / CONSTRAINTS -> OTHER FIRES / AFFECTED ENTITIES -> FEASIBLE CHOICES (INCLUDING WAIT/REFUSE) -> CONSEQUENCES / CARRIERS -> EVIDENCE / CORRECTION -> REVISIT`

Use it as **attention guidance**, not a mandatory worksheet or automatic STOP. A user facing a ten-minute deadline should not have to exhaustively map the city. They may take an already-authorised low-cost protective action; asking more questions can consume the remaining correction window. Where a domain expert or responsible actor already owns a reliable intervention, use them.

At each actual use, preserve the distinction between:

- **Observed, reported, inferred, disputed and unknown** (TRACE §6); source/access/independence are separate.
- **Exists, discoverable, known, usable and selected** (ME Appendix A and TRACE §12.2).
- **Can, may, should and will** (feasibility, authority, value, forecast).
- **Record corrected, threatened outcome protected, residue repaired and generating mechanism changed** (ME Parts II–IV).
- **One entity's improvement, another's loss, and a legitimate distribution of burdens.**

A good route can include chosen commitments that close alternatives; maintaining the greatest count of options is not the moral objective. A local gain or aggregate surplus cannot author the affected parties' consent.

## 3. What the independent returns actually earned

**Mathematics: useful exposure of assumptions, no superiority result.** Lane B's ordinary walking-home model makes the route/usable-policy gap, observation timing and incomparable cost vectors visible. Its household pool shows shared risk can improve an explicitly selected objective, shared failures can erase that gain, information timing may dissolve a voluntary agreement, and a later mandate can force redistribution. The results agree with existing partially observable planning and risk-sharing economics. Lane C's fixtures likewise reveal how changing permissions/goals/horizons changes the selected policy. These are valuable *teaching and audit examples*, **not** an improved algorithm compared with specialist methods.

**Proof/claim corrections that must survive integration:**

- Lane B's initial `r > 10/17` claim was **wrong**. In its specified model, the informative-stranger advantage holds for `max(r, 1-r) > 10/17`; an reliably wrong signal can be inverted.
- Probability recursion formerly divided by zero on probability-zero observation classes. The corrected model retains zero-prior worlds for a **robust** question but skips their probability mass in probabilistic recursion. This is a declared model convention, not a prior-free metaphysical rule.
- `K=0` is **loss/absence of a robust guarantee** at a knowledge state; favourable new information can restore that guarantee. It is **not permanent hardening**. Lane B's repaired `hardening_time()` is world-relative, uses an actual hidden world and all available model moves as a retrospective oracle, not something the bounded walker necessarily knows or can legitimately use. It is **not** a general protective remedy operator.
- Lane B's P4 initial-segment proof requires a stationary position and knowledge under a non-transforming null move; do not cite it as a theorem that real waiting cannot restore opportunities or that every moving/informing policy hardens monotonically.
- More freely available observations cannot reduce one actor's optimum **inside a fixed model with free observation refinement**. Asking, recording, disclosing and deliberating can take time, alter others' choices, or impose exposure. Do not generalise the free-information proposition across those changed dynamics.
- Coalition capability monotonicity in a **fixed** game under the stated controllable additional-player semantics does not imply membership, consent, equal benefit or more joint wellbeing is always available; changing coupling or participation conditions changes the game. The household case is a counterweight, not proof of benevolent combination.
- `Fractal` is not established; reapplication across scales must specify an abstraction relation and show that the property relied upon survives. `Least friction` without an order and `most efficient` without an actor, objective, costs and budget are unearned strong forms.

The harder unresolved fault is **epistemic hardening versus operational correction**. The toy distinguishes an observer's robust guarantee from an omniscient after-the-fact reachability status. ME's live timing claim additionally requires a *specific threatened thing*, an actually executable protective/remedy route, a possible actor with authority, competing claims, completion-time evidence, and a defensible loss-of-comparable-repair boundary. Neither toy establishes that whole chain. TRACE §9.1 already disciplines such strong window claims. Do not silently relabel goal arrival as ethical repair.
