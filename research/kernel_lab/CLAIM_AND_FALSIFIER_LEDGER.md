# Claim, authority and falsifier ledger

**ME v0.9 integrated kernel lab · Build, 13 October 2026 · NOT RELEASED / NOT VALIDATED / NOT A NEW ETHICS THEOREM**

The numbered claims are intentionally **narrow** and specify what observation or counterexample would withdraw them. Scope is *the authored finite lab or immutable cited research*, not actual universal laws. A meaningful ethical judgement requires domain evidence, affected people's standing, law, care and fair burden placement not solved here.

## 1. Claim classes

- **D — descriptive/mechanical**: property of a specified world and rules, conditional on explicit state, observation, exogenous transition and admitted possibilities. Its truth in a toy does not prove the toy fits life.
- **P — authored/contestable port**: entity designation, value/target, standing, horizon, risk, cost order, legitimate permission, consent, disclosure, fairness and intervention boundaries. These are not produced by an ordinary transition system.
- **E — evidence/provenance**: what was observed, reported, inferred, simulated, historical or unknown, at which time and under whose custody.
- **H — human/normative proposition**: human-facing invitation/commitment (care, good futures, dignity, honesty, refusal, appropriate protection). Could be defensible, but not derived by mechanics.
- **N — not regenerated**: rich human/technical content remains in existing complete releases or stronger external method, **not** reproduced by this little lab.

### One precise scale-collapse counterexample

Treat the two `betsy` variants as one macrostate because they have **identical actor-visible observation** at time0. Under action `block` they transition to `distress=1` and `distress=4`. If the abstraction claims a *single determinate distress outcome* for the macrostate, it is false. If it represents a set/probability over both outcomes and preserves the relevant observation policy, it may be useful but requires stronger abstraction/epistemic modelling. Merely reusing the same arrow diagram at both scales is not a fractal invariant, nor does a model of reaction assign moral standing.

### A real operational correction is not a reassuring trace

The `remedy` toy contains a world-side repair effect. It has **no verified time-to-completion, legal authority, cost distribution, access for the tenant or real-world intervention**. Before a real system may say 'protection is still possible', TRACE §9.1 requires a time origin, actor, available route, completion bound, relevant alternative, hardening boundary and evidence; otherwise `WINDOW_STATUS_UNKNOWN`. For a real person in active harm, the correct next step may be practical professional support, not another packet in this lab.
