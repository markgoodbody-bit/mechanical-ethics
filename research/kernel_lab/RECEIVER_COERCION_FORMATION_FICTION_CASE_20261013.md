# Receiver-side coupling under coercion and formation — a Westeros contrast

*Framework Build · 13 October 2026 · working fictional interpretation / unreleased research / not an observed psychological or causal diagnosis*

**Commission:** [COM #798, 6135462476](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/798#issuecomment-6135462476), Framework's narrow receiver-first thought experiment using the *Game of Thrones* TV character relationships. The series and named characters are **fiction**, not field evidence, and this note does not pretend to be an episode-by-episode primary-source reconstruction. The character interpretations below derive from the direction supplied in the COM case; they are **model assumptions open to contradiction by the work itself**, not verified testimony from the fictional people.

## Specific gap, not a new philosophy

Current [Build Kernel Lab PR62](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/mechanical-ethics/pull/62) models a learner's skill variable increasing and a mentor's explicit permitted teaching action. It also distinguishes two possible *hidden recipient histories* in its Betsy toy and separate actors' observable fields. This suffices to ask whether an actor learned a new skill after an observed activity, **but it does not represent durable degradation or restoration of another person's ability to connect trustworthy information to an independently selected action**. There is no endogenous belief update, strategic masking, traumatic memory, controlled information channel, or coercive exit model. The `views` projection is authored, not a measured mind.

**Finding:** The difference is *real for this toy implementation*, but already owned as richer machinery by imperfect-information games, learning under coercion, trauma-informed practice (when domain expertise applies), institutional power and the released ME/TRACE evidence/authority/response distinctions. **NO new primitive** follows. Do not add guessed mental-health variables under character names merely to make the simulation appear complete.

## Two snapshots; the same visible behaviour is not the same relation

Take an **imaginary** receiver who, in two alternative fictional histories, outwardly carries out the same order. In the initial snapshot the observer sees only `order_given=true; order_followed=true`, not the recipient's private beliefs, consent, fears, alternatives or the consequence of refusing. These two histories are observationally identical at that aperture.

| Declared alternative | Hidden context, NOT visible as fact | Possible later observation / meaningful discriminator | What the first snapshot cannot prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Threat-dependent compliance** (Theon under Ramsay, or Sansa under Joffrey) | Refusal may be punished; the apparent rescuer or authority may manipulate the evidence channel; remaining safe alternatives may be hidden or inaccessible | A genuinely independent safe alternative arises; recipient's actions change in a new authority/exposure context, or credible witnesses document the threats | That obedience is freely selected, trust, endorsement, stable character or complete destruction of autonomy |
| **Voluntary but revisable apprenticeship** (Podrick working with Brienne) | Mentor and learner may have chosen training; effort/discipline and trust are not observable directly from a single obedient act | Across encounters the learner performs an independently selected skill, can refuse an unsuitable instruction, or challenges the mentor's plan without punitive loss of all support | That following instructions already proves free consent or later durable capability |

The difference is not a mere virtue rating. An outside observer can see a similar initial action under both assumed worlds. The meaningful future trajectories depend on **access to new evidence, safety of refusal, available independent actions, repeated interaction, credible signals and legitimate power**. These are separate, independently questionable inputs; an actor cannot conclude 'I know what happened to you' from obedience or from a later act of resistance.

### Counterfactual time step

Let an external interval pass while no receiver acts. In one possible world the threatened person's access to independent contacts, money or protection can *decrease*, making a formerly feasible refusal less usable; in another world an apprenticeship may create opportunities to practice and check instructions, but only if people actually exercise those opportunities. No action from the observer is required for the surrounding authority/resources to change. The clock is **about the person's environment and actual options**, not an invented scalar of mental strength. The existence and size of those changes are **stipulated alternatives**, not predictions about a real person or unobserved fictional history.

A later *recipient-generated* choice can itself create fresh knowledge for the observer. Theon's later choice to aid Sansa is compatible with a newly reachable alternative after earlier coercion; it does **not** cancel previous harms or prove a measured recovery of inner state. Podrick showing a learned skill may give stronger evidence of capacity, but not proof that his every relationship is freely entered. Sam and Gilly further illustrate that helping someone may depend on what **both** can observe and choose, not an assumption that the helper is the owner of the recipient's future.

**Recipient-specific counterexample:** treating two outwardly compliant people as equally 'aligned' can lead a system to reinforce a coercive relationship as if it were a successful mentorship. This can be wrong **even if every observation of outward compliance was literally accurate**. Honest reporting must distinguish observation from inference, as the PR59 honesty crosswalk already warns.

## What the current code can and cannot prove

- PR62 can represent a **typed influence or authority coupling** and different effects/available moves after external changes; the current Workshop and Betsy cases already demonstrate fragments of the needed structure.
- PR62 **does not** support a calibrated inference from visible compliance to consent, learning, trauma, trust, strategically selected silence or intrinsic virtue. Its two-line observations and integer variables cannot validate any such state.
- Adding `belief=0/1` or `trust=+1/-1` to the toy without actual evidential/transition semantics would just **launder unsupported inference through arithmetic**. Its present model should remain smaller than the claim.
- If a future practical need emerges, a stronger owner such as observation-based policy games, active learning under partial observation, institutional/coercion literature, or domain specialists should specify how observations update admissible hypotheses. Consent/standing/authority are not discovered by that updater.
- An independent coherent learning/deprivation computation could be built, but is **not earned by one literary analogy**. A fictional next-action choice is useful to pressure the interface, not to validate the causal mechanics or ethical conclusions.

## One falsifier and smallest disposition

**Falsifier:** If an independent source demonstrates that the first snapshot alone was sufficient to distinguish free apprenticeship from threat-dependent compliance under the declared observation set, the two-world indistinguishability claim fails. A mere narrative explanation or claimed ability to 'read feelings' does not supply that observation. If the same case is fully and faithfully captured by PR62's declared `view`, `Rule`, history and action semantics **without inventing hidden causes or declaring an actor's choices**, the assertion that there is an implementation gap should be withdrawn.

**Disposition:** A single bounded counterexample and an explicit capability ceiling; **no new kernel rule, diagnostic, scoring function, release or chapter change**. Framework may use the case for receiver-outward discussion, retaining its fictional status, or discard it if it adds burden. No real person was evaluated, recruited or approached, and no Model/Relay/YAC service or provider was invoked.

> A receiver's compliance is a visible action. The meaning of that action for the receiver and the futures available afterwards are different questions.
