## Meaning of judgments — do not mistake term correspondence for derivation

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **D** | **DIRECT** textual structural correspondence already present in v0.9 Draft Zero (not a newly discovered theorem, nor an automatic normative implication) |
| **C** | **DERIVED ONLY CONDITIONAL** on supplied world model, observation function, affected scope, horizon, comparator, authority and/or value measure; state exactly which when making a claim |
| **P** | **REQUIRES AUTHORED PORT** — the human/normative/authority/distribution/evidence choice remains declared and contestable, never generated from neutral dynamics |
| **N** | **NOT REGENERATED** — source content, example, provenance, policy, humane commitment or special mechanism is not supplied by the small kernel and must remain available verbatim in the released source or be deliberately brought forward in future authoring |

These markers can combine. There are **no proven exact contradictions** simply from comparing prose in these files; the risks below are genuine **tensions to test**, not evidence that the released text was false. Actions **KEEP** = preserve substantive text/case/status; **TRANSFER** = possible future human-approved relocation with original source link; **CLARIFY** = make boundaries sharper; **CHALLENGE** = test, not delete.

## Tensions requiring honest test, not silent resolution

1. **Stop if insufficient authority** (draft loop step 6) must not forbid an independently authorised interim protective move during a contested assignment; neither does need create permission. Test against Codex's disputed-repair toy, not abstract virtue.
2. **“Least friction”** is an incomplete ordering unless costs, weights/thresholds/whose life and horizon are specified. Some costs are incommensurable; favor partial-order admission over a moral score.
3. **More joint possibilities** can coincide with coercion, surveillance or unequal burden. Saturday Workshop deliberately accepts fewer opening hours to preserve participants' agency. Compare both money/capability and protected refusal.
4. **Kernel `K(p,t)` correction** can mean model update, compensatory aftercare or timely protection; these are not equivalent. Keep ME `T_protect<T_harden` and TRACE exact window warrant.
5. **“Fractal”** describes visual analogy only until scale-mapping/invariants are demonstrated. Human, animal, institutional and machine scenes can be compared recursively without proving identical underlying mathematics.
6. **Ordinary use costs time.** A reader who has ten minutes or no spare money may be harmed by the instruction to map all alternatives. The navigation method needs a minimal-cost STOP/known-safe option, not compulsory exhaustive analysis.
7. **Positive possibility vs meaning.** A subjective feeling of hope, a supported prospect, a promised life, a life that has ended, an act of care and a later good are different claims. Do not merge them to rescue optimism.

## Proposed reader architecture (not edited manuscript)

- **Ground (brief):** a navigator in ordinary streets/paths, with limited view, time and effort; a destination is chosen, not imposed. Plain language, no notation, no morality theorem.
- **Other fires/others:** signals and needs outside the navigator's map. A question, offer, warning, crossing, or disagreement can be useful, unwanted, costly or coercive. Consent and legitimate authority are not obtained simply by drawing an arrow between people.
- **Choices/consequences:** action, wait, refusal and stopping occur while the world moves. Some wrong turns are reversible and some paths harden; compare feasible actual steps, not only attractive maps.
- **Correction/return:** a route needs relevant evidence at the right time; keeping a note may help a later navigator, but writing a note is not the same as fixing the road.
- **Bridge into Leah:** move to the existing released Leah-and-Mia damp/mould composite where institutional categories and human experience diverge. Keep the original scene, rather than rewriting it to illustrate every concept. Show how one process sees an input label and another lives with cough/shift/fear; unequal power changes whose clock and burden matters.
- **Reader freedom:** no mandatory checklist, no requirement to expose private experience, no obligation to adopt ME or TRACE, and no claim to know the reader's goals.
- **Main book route:** retain Part I access, II timing, III witness, IV machinery/predator, V honest closure and VI positive construction. Future structural reshuffling is a separate **human-authorised** editorial step only after no-loss reconciliation.

## Human cold-reader falsification — proposed, **NOT PERFORMED**

Use two readings matched for length/effort: released v0.8.0 opening (already entity-first) and a proposed navigator-first alternative (separate working draft), order randomised where appropriate. Do not use prior-project-primed team agreement as independent evidence; no external recruitment or model spend is authorised.

Ask readers in their own words, **without requiring disclosure of private experiences**:

1. What is the entity starting from; what is actually known, missing or only inferred?
2. What is it trying to do; who chose that direction and whose other future matters?
3. Is the world changing while the entity asks, waits or refuses? Give one ordinary example.
4. Which options really exist versus can actually be used? Who would pay time, attention, money or risk to use them?
5. What could another entity add or take away? Could a helpful offer become dependence or control?
6. Does the proposed text accidentally tell you to preserve every option, always cooperate, or obey the actor whose plan is best specified?
7. When could later correction be too late; does noting a mistake itself repair the harm?
8. What happens if you have only five minutes or cannot safely gather evidence?
9. Can you explain the Leah-and-the-wall scene *without* making the institution or Leah omniscient?
10. What could the author remove from this version without losing a material distinction? Which prior version was easier to use, and why?

Predeclare **failure** if readers systematically infer any of: `MORE_OPTIONS=GOOD`, `HELP=RIGHT_TO_CONTROL`, `UNKNOWN=FALSE`, `RECORD=REPAIR`, `NO_CLEAR_AUTHORITY=NO_PROTECTION_ACTION`, `FORMAL_ROUTE=USABLE`. Mark confusion, inability to bridge to an actual next step and reported analysis burden as valid negative results; do not equate articulate praise with efficacy.
