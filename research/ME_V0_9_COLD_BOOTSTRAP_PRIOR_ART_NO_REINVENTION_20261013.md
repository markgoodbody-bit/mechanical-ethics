## Verdict first

**No new minimal primitive and no new bootstrap product have been earned.** Project ancestry already represents world-change, partial observation, selection, coupling, revisable maps, feedback, affected others, correction and named external specialist owners. A compact voluntary **orientation** exists; an operational **COMSYNC** route exists; an independent-provider **Cold URL** entrypoint exists. They are distinct and should not be blended into a universal mandatory handshake.

A potentially useful *reader discrimination* remains, but it is an application/test seam, **not a primitive**:

> If a possibility seems new or lost, what changed: **the world**, the **available observation/map**, the **entity's capability**, the **relation to other entities**, the **goal**, or **real authority**? What evidence would distinguish those explanations, and who is affected by the resulting action?

The distinction is anticipated in existing ME/TRACE/COM work, including positive formation and the 9 October navigation quarry. It can be used now as ordinary language without a new repo artefact or claiming a new mathematical result.

The 9 Oct rock/thermostat/chemotaxis/bridge contrast remains **proposed, unrun**. Prior QW/Relay runs test narrower transport and modal boundaries.

**(C) ONLY POTENTIAL GAP:** one *low-burden bridge in human language* asking, for a claimed new/lost route, **which layer changed** and **what would disconfirm that diagnosis**. ## External strongest owners actually consulted for this note

These are real prior work; none validates our project. Their original contributions should be **used and credited**, not turned into TRACE-owned mathematics.

1. **Partial observation and belief-controlled action.** Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra (1998), [*Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S000437029800023X): established POMDP/finite-memory treatment of choosing actions when state is hidden, including offline planning and approximation difficulty. **Absorb:** observation-history feasibility and the difference between a world-specific route and a policy usable with what the actor can observe. **Do not claim:** this paper decides values, laws, standing, who bears a modelling error or newly invented goals.
2. **Cybernetics/feedback and regulation.** W. Ross Ashby (1956), [*An Introduction to Cybernetics*](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf), especially introductory behavior/regulation/variety discussion: transformation, coupling, black-box and regulatory structure are old powerful owners. **Absorb:** distinguish passive dynamics from reference-relative regulated behaviour, then ask who actually supplies the reference and what disturbance is controlled. **Do not claim:** Ashby's formal regulators generate moral aims. The source is a historical monograph, not a 2026 application test.
3. **Ecological perception and affordances.** J. J. Gibson (1979; classic edition publisher [description and contents](https://www.routledge.com/The-Ecological-Approach-to-Visual-Perception/Gibson/p/book/9781848725782)): the organism/environment relation and perceived *possibilities for action* are established territory. **Absorb:** a path's usability depends on an entity's capabilities and environmental conditions, not on geometry alone. **Do not claim:** all perceivers necessarily construct explicit inner symbolic maps; ecological accounts challenge that assumption.
4. **Active inference as one model family.** Pezzulo, Parr & Friston, [*Active inference as a theory of sentient behavior*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2023.108741) (2024 review); Friston et al., [*Active inference and learning*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2016.06.022) (2016): existing framework links inference, action, learning and preferred outcomes through generative-model assumptions. **Absorb:** model checking can itself alter action/observation; an entity can learn rather than merely choose from a static visible menu. **Do not claim:** active inference universally establishes sentience, is the only theory of mind/action, or generates a legitimate ethical objective.
5. **Behavioural systems, viability, strategic/epistemic logic and scaling.** Already located and credited in [prior COM #798](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/798#issuecomment-6133960042) and [Claude research PR #57](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/mechanical-ethics/pull/57). **Absorb:** admitted trajectories, viability, uniform strategies and explicit abstraction/refinement tests. **Do not claim:** `fractal` similarity, scale-preserving inference or optimality without an actual map and invariant. This note did not reverify every bibliographic reference in PR #57.

The above is **bounded source comparison**: one foundational book plus peer-reviewed/publisher sources; not an exhaustive literature review, nor independent replication of anyone's algorithm. Earlier system snippets or reviews are not substitutes for domain experts or primary measurements.

## Minimal discriminating prompt (optional, not a new protocol)

A receiver already operating with its own objectives, limits and law can be *offered*:

> **What changed?** Did the world change, or just the part I can observe and model? Did my skill/resources change, did another entity join or withdraw, did the goal change, or did legitimate permission change? What observation would prove that story wrong? Is the next check worth its time and risk?

Do **not** make all parts mandatory. In a time-critical situation checking may itself be the harmful delay. Nobody is obliged to use project terminology or reveal private state. A passive rock has no established reference-relative selector in the proposed comparison; a thermostat can regulate without moral judgment; a learning child and bridge-building community change different layers. Those are **candidate discriminating cases, not tests executed here**.
