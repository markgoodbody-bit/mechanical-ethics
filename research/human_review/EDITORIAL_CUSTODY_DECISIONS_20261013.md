## 3. Explicit comparison against the existing v0.9 drafts

**PR58 — Framework human editorial reconciliation.** Keep its main judgements: v0.8 opening already entity-first, Workshop already constructive, new human entrance must not push people through two map metaphors, older fields own theoretical mathematics, reader effect unmeasured. This candidate makes an **editorial experiment with a different entrance**—Workshop first—and unifies one positive outcome with one pressured protective outcome, instead of adding another city/traveller setup. It does not claim to outperform PR58 without real reader feedback.

**PR59 — Build full research manuscript.** Keep that manuscript intact for sections on Arthur, Rosa, Amina, human witness, machine complexity, other fires, residue and multi-party constraints. Here deliberately **remove** the second map journey, several repeated navigation recap chapters, mathematical sidebars and the narrative burden of re-telling every original scene. The shorter candidate follows a through-line: *repair → skill → threatened room → practical route and clock → other person's agency → honest witness → correction and creation*. The smaller text **loses details** and is not interchangeable with either full text. The full manuscript custody map (55/34 headings) remains necessary.

**PR61 / PR62 / CC work.** Keep only rigorously supported editorial limits: exogenous time even under null, observation history matters, route-existence is weaker than an observation-usable policy, simulated capability ≠ observed learning, all permissions/values are authored, earlier PR62 same-tick actor order required a concrete patch, \`K=0\` does not imply irreversible hardening, and no universal cross-scale wisdom/fractal invariant is proved. No mathematical chapter is added to a human review simply because code passed tests.

**Owner-first field material.** Existing [Housing Ombudsman guidance](https://www.housing-ombudsman.org.uk/centre-for-learning/support-guides/damp-and-mould/) and [UK government Awaab's Law guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords-timeframes-for-repairs-in-the-social-rented-sector) own real social-housing pathways; the v0.8 Leah/Mia account is a composite and does not imply statutory status or replace these sources. Existing [Repair Café International](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/about/) and [house rules](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/house-rules/) already explain practical volunteer repair, learning, risk and refusal better than a new ME intake form. The editorial review treats each field as a contrast, **not** an ME-branded replacement process.

- Two matched-burden new readers of the **existing released source** and this shorter review understand the creation-versus-delivery and file-versus-life distinctions equally well, with no practical change: **NO DELTA**; keep v0.8 alone.
- A reader mistakes the composite Workshop or Leah/Mia for reported observations: **REVISE** explicit source status.
