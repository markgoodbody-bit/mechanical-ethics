## Reader route and conceptual dependency

The text starts with definitions in plain English before the first main illustration and uses a small number of honest, conditional compressions instead of repeated formula cards. Reader learns a **state** of selected features and relevant **change** across moments, with actual correspondence of the same studied feature; a selected **boundary** never appoints moral standing. An **aperture** is an access position and an **account** a scoped representation with observed/inferred/unknown status. When a relevant feature changes and an account lacks present warrant, the *current-use claim* may need rechecking; age by itself does not establish falsehood.

An **action** is distinguished from execution and realised effect; null, delay and refusal require evidence. **Carrier/coupling** link distinct actors/conditions without common intent or ownership. A **route** is not yet a usable prospect without ability, time, permissions, information and resources. The never-offered target set is named, so a missing rejection is not proof of opportunity. A two-streets illustration distinguishes one route for each imagined world from a single observation-based policy warranted across the considered uncertainty. A sign can increase knowledge; a bridge can create a usable route.

A **clock** and control/exposure asymmetry determine whether an actual **effect** can arrive before the specifically named path hardens. The timing inequality is a *necessary selected-path condition*, not proof of efficacy, permission or normative duty. Unknown timing remains UNKNOWN. **Value, standing and authority** are declared and contestable commitments at the moral interface, never mechanically derived. **Comparison** requires scope, baseline, evidence, horizon and their authors. **Witness/custody/answerability** require a lawful usable path to someone capable of effect. Feedback, closure, apology and timestamps alone do not certify remedy; obstruction and retaliation risks remain real route conditions. **Correction** is distinguished from **restoration** and residue; the next person's route tests whether a mechanism changed rather than a single case being closed.

## Generative/positive compositions actually earned

**Early: another aperture to learning.** A child who disturbs a seed to check it gains a different, checkable method from a neighbour, if it reaches the child and is independently tried. A new account and an appropriate action can improve knowledge without the neighbour assuming authority over the child. The outcome is conditional: access, willingness and verification matter.

**Mid: carrier, practice and feedback to formation.** A method transmitted across a relationship, safe attempts, correction and evidence of performance on a changed task can support a retained skill. Repetition/obedience alone cannot prove that skill. Its availability still depends on maintenance and the person's agency.

**Late: shared capability and supported prospect.** A repair workspace can be made through several holders (room, tools, skill, scheduling and legitimate protections) none of whom has the whole arrangement alone. Maintenance, burdens, replacement skills, consent and refusal are integral. A supported prospect is an evidenced, revisable route rather than a demanded feeling of hope. A shared capability can fail or exploit; it is not automatically morally beneficial. Kindness is represented as burden placement toward a willing capable bearer rather than praise of the helper or taking possession of the helped person.

The **adverse composition** is separately earned: relevant state change + past limited account + unsupported present-use warrant + attempted action can create a missed or blocked route; even correct feedback can fail to reach legitimate action or effect in time. Obstruction is an observable property of the route, not inferred malicious intent.
