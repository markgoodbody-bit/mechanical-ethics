## Conscious exclusions and unsettled foundations

The body does **not** import a formal TRACE schema, executable ports, a chapter-by-chapter loss calculation, mathematical probability model, mandated checklists, an adversarial benchmark, or all the original human stories. Those remain accessible in released v0.8, TRACE and research. This is a human concept-dependency narrative, not a claim that those specialised tools are dispensable. No exact judgement of normative standing, multi-party priority, ecological representation or legitimate closure has been derived; chapter 6 and later discussions deliberately leave these as declared and disputed commitments.

A moral collision cannot always be solved by reopening more doors. An irreversible event may leave no continuing impairment; a new capability may form without constituting ethical gain. A better future is a commitment and field of possibilities, not a mathematical consequence of the substrate.
