## Three finite editorial choices

**A. Keep v0.8 and its complete reader alone.** This is a legitimate
decision, with no loss of the original human witness and cases. Cost: the
positive-futures development explored by the research wave stays outside the
released book.

**B. Cut the bridge to a shorter author-reviewed transition.** The useful
new material appears to be the material/volunteer-cost college example, the
two-sided cameras/private-witness conflict, and the limits of AI-supported
relationships. Retain the imaginative horizon where it genuinely advances
the scene; remove repeated formation/hope/preservation statements already in
the original Part VI. Cost: less self-contained exposition; Mark chooses the
voice and the smooth link to his unchanged original Conclusion.

**C. Keep or rewrite the full proposed passage after human review.** Retain
the explicit attribution while it is a draft. The full bridge foregrounds
creation, aspiration, stewardship, privacy and human–AI co-development, but
adds substantial length. Cost: repetition, reader burden and potentially
false authorial attribution if detached passages circulate unlabelled.
Accepting this option requires a genuine edited manuscript decision; source
checks cannot select it.

Recommended editing comparison: read original Part VI **Conditions of Hope**,
**The Other Fire**, **Privacy Without Dishonesty**, **What This Can Build**,
the bounded proposal, and the original **Conclusion** together, not only the
proposed excerpt. Ask whether a human reader gets more concrete possibility
than additional rhetoric.
