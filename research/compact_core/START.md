# Mechanical Ethics: a compact kernel and language candidate

Editor-review proposal, 13 October 2026. Codex Windows. Based on the complete
[released v0.8 book](../../MECHANICAL_ETHICS.md) and [PR65's decision map](../integrated_wave/ARCHITECTURE.md).
The book, figures, author voice and TRACE remain unchanged. This is a proposed
reusable core, not a released edition or text attributed to Mark.

## Start with the situation, not the form

Something matters to someone in a changing situation. They can see part of it,
act on part of it, and depend on other people or arrangements for the rest.
The world keeps moving while they act, wait, refuse or look for help.

Begin at that middle: **what is changing for this being, and what could actually
change it next?** Look outward to those affected and those able to act. Look
inward when a component's different information, power or clock matters. Change
scale deliberately; an organisation's completed task can leave a person's needed
capability unrestored.

Use only the distinctions needed for the next consequential choice:

1. **Boundary and partial map.** Who is in this account, who is missing, and
   what does each know? Keep observation, report, inference, dispute and unknown
   separate. A model can be useful and mistaken at the same time.
2. **Moves and authority.** What can each actually do—including wait or refuse?
   Which moves are legitimately available? Capability is not permission. A
   claimed permission can be contested; a model flag cannot settle that dispute.
3. **Transition and affected future.** What changes under the move and what
   changes anyway? Who gains access or skill; who loses time, privacy, money or
   safety? Keep each affected party visible rather than hiding them in a total.
4. **Clock and correction.** What still matters by when? Identify an actor,
   reachable route and completion evidence. A corrected record, protection of a
   threatened outcome, repair of residue and change to the generating mechanism
   are four different results.
5. **Witness and answerability.** What could show this account wrong, who can
   preserve or challenge it, and can the challenge change future action? A hash
   checks correspondence, not truth. An apology or complaint reference may matter
   without establishing a practical correction.
6. **Formation.** What could become possible through learning, construction,
   maintenance or a chosen commitment? Distinguish an existing route newly found,
   a skill newly acquired, and a shared arrangement newly built. Participation
   and refusal remain real; helpful behaviour does not prove an inner character.

These are attention prompts, not six mandatory tasks. If a known, authorised
protective action is time-critical, more mapping can be the harmful delay. Use
the responsible domain owner where they already offer the simpler useful route.
Do not make the person in difficulty carry an institution's coordination work.

## What the kernel can and cannot establish

A represented model can specify states, partial observations, moves and
transitions. Once targets, horizons, uncertainty treatment, resource limits and
permissions are supplied, established planning methods can test conditional
reachability. The retained reference implementations do that in deliberately
small synthetic worlds; they neither discover real-world authority nor judge
whose life should take priority.

The bindings remain explicit: **designation, legitimacy, target, horizon,
measure over unseen worlds, cost order, standing and aggregation**. The final
pair is grouped in CC's B7; this document does not turn it into a solved rule.
Protected floors, competing claims and legitimate closure still need a defensible
normative/domain process. If the selected constraints conflict, report that
conflict rather than quietly relaxing one to produce an answer.

For precision, let `M` be the stated model, `h` the actor's observation/action
history, `B` the declared bindings and `pi(h)` an observation-usable policy.

```text
for each admitted world, some successful route exists
does not imply
one permitted policy using available observations succeeds across those worlds.
```

These are established imperfect-information planning distinctions, not ME
theorems. A probabilistic question additionally needs an explicit prior and
threshold; it is not interchangeable with a robust question over admitted worlds.

Likewise, **loss of a robust model guarantee is not actual-world hardening**.
New information can restore that guarantee. Goal arrival in a toy is not repair
of harm. For a real protective claim, the book's comparison between effective
protection and hardening needs common clock origin, comparable units, feasible
completion, authority, alternatives and evidence. Without those, the practical
window remains unknown. Naming the unknown does not itself help the person.

Repeated questions can be used at different scales. Their answers transfer only
with a stated mapping that preserves the relevant observations, actions and
property. Similar diagrams do not establish fractal invariance or inherited
standing. More routes, more money and more power are not automatic improvement;
a worthwhile commitment can deliberately close options.

## Use the cases, then try to break the reading

[Five complete cases and one reported challenge](CASES.md) show the whole loop:
boundary → partial map → move → transition → future/clock → witness →
answerability → formation. Each ends with a concrete question or source-bounded
next step, a negative control and an explicit limit—not an instruction to a real
person to comply with a model.

Run the existing instruments through the thin example adapter:

```text
python -m research.compact_core.examples
python -m unittest research.compact_core.test_examples -v
python -m research.integrated_wave.check_wave
```

No new planner, glyph or universal case-input schema is introduced. The adapter
uses the retained PR65 engines, labels their results synthetic, and refuses to
promote them to real protection or legitimate authority. TRACE remains optional
and separate; plain English comes first.

Earned: executable, auditable distinctions inside specified models and preserved
source custody. Proposed: this core may make those distinctions easier to transfer.
Unresolved: usefulness beyond careful ordinary thought, reader burden, real causal
fit, normative foundation, standing, collision and floor-grounding. The complete
v0.8 book is the comparator; the stronger specialist may be the better next step.
