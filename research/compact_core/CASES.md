# Five whole cases and a reported external challenge

Read [START](START.md) first. Book scenes are composites. Numerical tests below
are separate authored models, not measurements of the people in those scenes.
The source-custody chain and known owners are retained in PR65, especially CC's
mathematical reference and the original full v0.8 narratives.

## 1. Leah and Mia: protection before a file finishes

**Boundary/map:** Leah sees the changing room and Mia's cough; the portal sees
a category, timestamp and photograph. Neither account is complete. A report
records the request; it does not establish the hazard's cause or legal category.

**Move/transition/clock:** report, wait, seek another route, and responsible
protection are different actions. The room can change during all of them.
The scene supplies no measured protective deadline or independently verified
intervention. A fixture may stipulate such a deadline; it must not lend that
number back to the narrative.

**Witness/answerability:** ask the responsible organisation who can change the
physical condition, what interim protection they can legitimately deliver, and
what observation would establish completion. Keep the affected account reachable
through later decisions. A file correction can leave disruption and residue.

**Formation:** learn and repair the organisational handoff for the next resident,
but do not make Leah learn a theory before protection. The separate low-reserves
fixture gives the same answer as ordinary reasoning: use the already-known,
permitted timely action rather than spend the window mapping alternatives.

**Challenge:** remove that action's permission or actual availability. The model
must no longer claim it succeeds. A complaint reference must not count as repair.
Domain guidance and responsible professional action are stronger owners; this
case offers no legal or medical advice and no verified remedy.

## 2. Saturday Workshop: an object, a skill and a shared capacity

**Boundary/map:** Nia brings a faulty bicycle; Priya and Mick bring skills, tools
and time. Idris's working environment matters. The complete composite includes
privacy, sponsorship terms, refusal, cost and people left on a waiting list.

**Move/transition/clock:** repair can make the bicycle usable now. Repeated
practice can make a later repair independently usable by Nia. Teaching and
maintaining tools/place can give others a continuing route. A single repair count
does not establish all three outcomes; a scripted skill label is not observed
human learning.

**Witness/answerability:** ask what the participant can later initiate, inspect,
question or refuse without the helper's possession of the outcome. Accounts of
progress must retain costs as well as gains, and participants can challenge their
records. Do not infer consent from attendance or development from obedience.

**Formation:** in the joint lab, learning precedes a separately permitted teaching
step. Same-tick practice cannot silently satisfy teaching's prerequisite. That
tests a causal contract, not the truth of the human scene.

**Challenge:** remove permission or delay the capability prerequisite. Teaching
must be unavailable. Compare the full sponsor-refusal scene: more funding creates
capacity but can impose control. The existing capability/learning disciplines
own the substantive methods; no new training programme is earned here.

## 3. Conflicting actors: refuse to hide the floor

**Boundary/map:** a shared service gives one participant capacity while imposing
unwanted control or burden on another. The modeller knows the chosen limits; it
does not discover an affected person's legitimate rights.

**Move/transition/clock:** staying preserves a stipulated joint-capacity outcome;
separating protects a stipulated personal outcome but costs more effort. Waiting
has consequences too. In the fixture, changing the authored target changes the
selected route. Applying a zero imposed-burden limit excludes the capacity route.

**Witness/answerability:** show both outcomes and which binding chose between
them. If the constraints cannot jointly be met, expose the conflict. Do not
automatically raise the burden limit or call the excluded party unreasonable.
The next useful question is who has legitimate standing to settle the conflict,
with what opportunity for affected parties to answer back.

**Formation:** a revised arrangement might permit joint benefit later, but the
present model does not contain one. Record that as an absent represented option,
not proof that a real alternative is physically impossible.

**Challenge:** delete the target. Output must remain unknown, not default to the
author's preferred morality. The fixture's budget is an authored constraint, not
grounding for a universal protected floor; collision settlement stays open.

## 4. Institution and person: the aggregate hides a route

**Boundary/map:** an institutional dashboard says each admitted circumstance has
some route. A person lacks the distinguishing information needed to choose one
before the only consequential step. Departmental availability and actor-usable
access are not the same property.

**Move/transition/clock:** the retained office fixture has world-specific routes,
yet no robust observation-usable policy when information arrives late. Earlier
information changes that result. This is an imperfect-information example, not
a simulation of an actual bureaucracy or proof that every institution fails.

**Witness/answerability:** ask which department owns the complete access outcome,
which actor can identify and execute the route, and whether the handoff reaches
them in time. Compare actual completion at the affected-person boundary with
the institution's summary. A useful witness preserves that discrepancy so it can
alter a future handoff rather than merely become another statistic.

**Formation:** design a repeatable handoff and reduce dependency on insider IDs
or exceptional persistence. That design is a proposed next step, not implemented
institutional reform. No-clear-chooser responsibility and distributed authority
remain unresolved.

**Challenge:** do not let the policy inspect the hidden world. Coarse summaries
may preserve route existence while losing usability. Transfer across scales
requires a property-specific mapping; recursive questioning alone is no proof.
Established information-design/planning methods own the mechanics.

## 5. Stale evidence: inheritance is not present access

**Boundary/map:** an old note says to take the left route. Its prior truth and
currentness are distinct. A confident inherited instruction is not authority.

**Move/transition/clock:** in the authored Game worlds, following the note succeeds
in one and fails in another. Checking a stipulated reliable current sign, then
acting on that observation, reaches the stated goal in both within this fixture's
budget. A costly or unreliable check would need another model.

**Witness/answerability:** retain the old note, current observation, action and
outcome separately. Reconstructing a journal catches a fabricated result even
after someone recomputes its hash. That establishes consistency with the model,
not an authenticated real event or independent witness.

**Formation:** change the future source-checking practice if the actual failure
recurs and the check buys more than it costs. Do not prescribe checking everything
forever; carelessly added enquiry can consume a protective window.

**Challenge:** change the world consequence while keeping the visible sign, or
make the check too late. The original policy's guarantee must not be exported
to that new world. Current source truth remains an evidence question.

## Outside pressure: telephone restoration versus restored access

Mark supplied a transcript of [Lindybeige's account](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9YASgAeCoM).
Independent video fetching was throttled. The speaker reports that losing the
original number blocked account authentication and banking, while a temporary
number restored some calls without restoring those dependencies. He reports
conflicting provider accounts and a pending transfer order blocking a tariff
change. These are reported claims, not verified provider facts or legal findings.

The discriminating question is **what capability is restored at the person's
boundary?** Telephone connectivity, account access, payment control and affordable
outgoing calls can each have different owners and clocks. The customer becoming
the messenger between organisations is a burden, not evidence of a usable repair
route. No deadline, fault assignment or provider contact is invented here.

This pressure case earns no new THR record, legal guidance or numerical fixture.
It challenges the compact language to name a whole dependency and an accountable
route rather than reward a component ticket's completion. Whether the language
actually reduces this person's burden remains unknown.
