# Selected kernel, human language and failure map

This is Codex's integration judgement over the pinned sources in `check_wave.py`,
not a new theorem or a change to the released book. Claims below have three
different owners: stipulated mechanics, authored/evidential bindings, and human
interpretation. A result in the first does not establish the other two.

## The centre in plain English

Begin with someone or something in a changing situation. What crosses its
boundary is only part of what is happening. A useful next move depends on what
it can actually do, what it may legitimately do, what matters to the affected
parties, and what will change while it acts, waits or refuses.

The useful question is not always which existing route to choose. A route might
need to be found, a skill learned, a shared arrangement built, or a useful capacity
maintained. Those are different changes. Equally, a chosen commitment can close
options for a worthwhile purpose. More options is not itself a moral objective.

This orientation is optional attention guidance. Where a usable urgent protective
route already exists, it should not demand a longer analysis from the person who
needs it. Responsibility for evidence, protection and mechanism repair should
not disappear into a worksheet placed on that person.

## Architecture: keep three layers separate

| Layer | Selected source structure | What it cannot supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Represented dynamics | Time, states, designated actors and moves, transitions, observations, admitted initial worlds; history-dependent policies | Truth of a real causal account; complete possibilities; consciousness or rights |
| Explicit bindings and evidence | Designation, legitimacy, targets, horizon, measure over unseen worlds, cost order, standing/aggregation; dated observation and custody | A legitimate authority merely because a flag says allowed; grounded welfare or fair collision resolution |
| Human language and action | Existing full scenes; feasible protection, meaningful refusal, formation, privacy, burden, answerability and hope | Universal optimisation; compelled disclosure; a remedy or better outcome by saying the words |

Use CC's finite concurrent model as an attributed specialist specification.
Navigator/Game instantiate a narrower deterministic hidden-initial-world model.
Build's lab executes preselected joint actions; it is not an observation-policy
solver. **These implementations are not interchangeable.** Do not write adapters
that erase their information, timing or authority contracts merely to produce a
single-looking output.

Paths and policies are conditional derivations. A typed interaction graph may be
derived after choosing entity boundaries. Care, witness independence, standing,
protected floors, fair burden placement and legitimate closure remain additional
human/evidential/normative problems. Putting them in an input port neither solves
them nor makes them dispensable.

This ledger overrides no original source. It tells the integrated reviewer which
reading to use and what to challenge. Historical mistakes remain inspectable in
their source history rather than being silently edited out of a contributor's
account.

The portable contribution being proposed is an inspectable set of distinctions
and source relationships. Against established planning and risk-sharing methods,
the existing computed cases show **no incremental algorithmic advantage**. Against
ordinary careful explanation, practical advantage and lower reader burden remain
unmeasured. Do not manufacture comparative success from weak baseline heuristics.

## One wave-level falsification plan

First run the integrated custody/regression command. A mismatch or error is a
source integration problem, not an objection to be explained away. Then peer
review the selected interpretations above, especially P2/P4/P5's unchanged-model
conditions and the operational remedy gap. Preserve the full book as comparator.

The consequential human review questions are: can an ordinary reader identify
one usable next action or meaningful refusal, without more burden; distinguish
building capability from mere instruction compliance; and reject a misleading
record/clock/authority claim? Those questions require actual use evidence if a
benefit claim is made. No outside participants, provider evaluation or contact
are authorised or performed by this package.

For a whole successor edition, Mark still chooses whether to integrate a compact
formation bridge, retain only a companion, or keep v0.8 unchanged. Source custody
and descriptive mathematics do not make that authorial choice. Standing,
collision and floor-grounding remain unresolved rather than being closed by a
new version number.
