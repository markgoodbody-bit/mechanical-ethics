# Two Clocks v3: a quieter illustrated-book register

One optional candidate responding to Mark's actual feedback that v2 was cleaner but too minimal (COM828 c6194754841). The change is in the drawing's balance and register, not in its information. All earlier alternatives and the manuscript are preserved.

**What changed.** The row labels now occupy a consistent right-aligned column rather than sitting loosely over the tracks. Three event labels share an upper baseline, while the housing loss sits beneath the event it names. Installed Georgia brings a modest serif texture; italic row labels separate the two paths without heavier type. A small copper fill draws the eye to the shared actual effect. Rounded rule ends and a light same-event guide soften the geometry without adding a new symbol, frame, flourish, house icon or piece of explanation. Colour carries no unique meaning: dots and ticks remain distinct in monochrome.

**What did not change.** Ten visible label words, six labels, no bold type or UNKNOWN panel, one common start, the same usable payment on both paths, payment-related hardening later and housing loss earlier. Smallest label remains approximately 11.91pt at 126mm width; row labels are approximately 12.70pt. Graphic height is 59.5mm (v2 54.6mm), allowing a little more breathing room rather than shrinking text. The top labels are aligned for visual order, not to assert equal-duration intervals.

## Book caption (unchanged from v2)

The same funds can become usable before one loss hardens, but after a home is lost. This is an illustrative ordering; acknowledgement is omitted because it is not the effect being compared.

## Accessible alternative

**Short alt:** The same funds become usable before a payment-related loss hardens, but after a coupled housing loss; both paths use one start.

**Long description:** Two unscaled horizontal tracks are labelled Payment and Housing in an aligned left column. A common vertical origin starts both. Two copper-filled circles, joined by a thin vertical guide, show one funds-usable event at the same time on both tracks. The upper loss tick is later; the lower home-loss tick is earlier. Round versus straight marks preserve this relation without colour. The guide identifies the same event shown twice, not a third event or evidence of causality. The order is hypothetical and unmeasured. Administrative receipt or acknowledgement is deliberately omitted; neither is the actual effect being compared.

**Precision belongs in the surrounding body.** DEV2 Chapter 6 retains strict `T(protect, a) < T(harden, a)` as necessary, not sufficient; common evidenced starts, declared and contestable loss criteria, separate coupled effects, UNKNOWN neither automatic holding nor continuation, competent authority and exposure created by holds. This candidate neither removes those qualifications nor repeats them as graphic clutter. Geometry shows an ordering, not a completed protection, restoration or domain-authority judgement.
