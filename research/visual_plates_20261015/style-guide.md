# Visual system — three plates, one editorial language

**Purpose.** Make a relationship visible where prose otherwise asks the reader to hold several positions at once. Do not decorate a warning or pretend a drawing has demonstrated its claim.

**Size and type.** The book PDF preference is **A4 portrait, UK English**. Each SVG is 126mm × 106.4mm (900 × 760 units), fitting within the proposed 150mm single-column measure; it was also checked at this narrower width rather than relying on enlargement. Working proof: installed Arial, then Helvetica, then sans-serif, not a final publication font. No Source Sans 3, Source Serif 4 or DejaVu files were found in the system/user Windows font locations checked. Their availability/licences on the final publishing system remain to be confirmed; no download or substitution is silently certified. Live editable text, not outlined glyphs. Minimum label 26 units ≈ 10.3pt at 126mm width; title 44 units ≈ 17.5pt. Keep captions outside the plate at the publication body's reading size. Do not shrink a whole plate to fit a narrow column.

**Palette and linework.** FW's provisional charcoal `#27312F`, paper white; the sole chromatic accent is deep teal `#27584F`, with rare sage wash `#E8EFEB`. Copper/slate are intentionally unused to retain the original one-accent brief. Monochrome exports replace both ink and accent with neutral `#242424` and wash with `#f1f1f1`. No category, certainty or moral status depends on colour. Structural lines 2.5 units (≈1pt); light ground lines 1.5 units. No gradients, shadows or ornamental effects.

**Legend.** Plate 1: hollow circle = acknowledgement; filled circle = funds usable; vertical tick = specified loss hardening; shared vertical rule = common declared origin. Positions show only the illustrative order, not durations. Plate 2: page outline = selected account, not world boundary; arrow = information/challenge route to review, not causal sufficiency, guaranteed transmission, authority transfer or compulsory veto. Plate 3: repeated window/table/glass/pot/child = continuity of the illustrative scene; an additional pot suggests a different task, not proved transfer. There are **no dashed connections**; UNKNOWN is written, not encoded as a speculative event on an axis.

**Composition.** Low label count; no tiny prose boxes or implied scores. Use neutral actors and ordinary objects. Retain negative space around the core mechanism. The plant panels are schematic, not a commissioned scene painting. Their caption must keep practice, resources, maintained access and voluntary choice visible.

**Accessibility and source.** Each SVG has a unique `<title>` and `<desc>`, a real viewBox and `role="img"`; long descriptions/captions are supplied separately for print and HTML publication. Captions name the source edition, exact line anchors and interpretive additions. A metadata test is not an assistive-technology result. Check text fallback, black-and-white output and actual reading width after any placement.

**Editorial gate.** These remain optional candidates. Mark approves style/placement; Build changes prose; Framework resolves interpretation. New source revisions require a source-map check. Never infer actual learning, timely protection or safe recourse merely because its diagram rendered correctly.
