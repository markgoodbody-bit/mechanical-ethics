# Two Clocks v2: less ink, more space

Optional alternative responding to Mark's actual page-3 feedback, COM828 c6194085474. The original PR76 figure and complete reviewed A4 proof are preserved. No manuscript, book PDF or other plate has been altered.

## Proposed book caption

The same funds can become usable before one loss hardens, but after a home is lost. This is an illustrative ordering; acknowledgement is omitted because it is not the effect being compared.

## Beside the figure / body context

The order is illustrative, not measured time. The body's strict `T(protect, a) < T(harden, a)` condition remains necessary, not sufficient: a timing comparison neither establishes authority nor decides whether an action is justified. Both clocks need a common evidenced start and a declared, contestable loss criterion. Where the needed evidence is missing, the window remains UNKNOWN; that alone orders neither holding nor continuing. A hold can change the clocks and expose someone else. These conditions remain in DEV2 Chapter 6; this note is an integration reminder, not replacement manuscript prose.

## Removed from the graphic

- Duplicate large title and introductory sentence. The A4 page can supply a heading.
- Bold path headings and bold focal-event text. All six labels are regular weight.
- The acknowledgement event and its label, whose distinction is now explicit in the caption.
- The second explanation of the same funds and of housing already lost.
- Boxed UNKNOWN warning, repeated strict-before slogan and colour wash.
- The extra t0 notation/legend. One shared starting rule and plain label remain.

The two tracks, common origin, usable-effect dots, two loss ticks and their order are retained. The vertical hairline joins the two dots because they represent the same payment at the same time; it is not a third event, a duration measurement or proof of causality. Dots mean usable funds; ticks mean the stated loss point. No colour-only category or dashed uncertainty mark is used.

## Accessible alternative

**Short alt:** One payment arrives before a payment-related loss hardens, but after a coupled housing loss; both tracks use the same start.

**Long description:** Time reads from left to right on two unscaled horizontal tracks beginning at one vertical starting rule. The upper track is Payment and the lower Housing. Two aligned filled dots are joined by a light vertical guide and represent the same funds becoming usable. On the upper track the loss-hardening tick is to the right of that event; on the lower track the home-loss tick is to its left. Receiving a request is not represented as protection. This synthetic ordering makes no claim that a particular payment, deadline or tenant case was measured. It does not settle other risks, authority, restoration or what to do under uncertainty.
