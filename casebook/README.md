## Why work through a case?

An idea becomes easier to challenge when we can see what it makes somebody
notice, what it leaves out and which practical decision might change.
Mechanical Ethics proposes a language for doing that across different settings.
It does not supply every setting's facts, laws, values or competent judgement.

This supplement follows three deliberately different questions. What happens
when information supplied to one public institution becomes useful to another
with a different purpose? How does knowledge of a physical danger become
protection that people can actually use? What would make a first paid step into
technical work a supported possibility rather than a promise?

These are not three proofs of the same answer. A lawful disclosure decision
cannot be settled by an evacuation model, and a learning arrangement cannot be
judged by the number of forms it completes. The useful connection is the work
of tracing circumstances, evidence, power, timing and achieved effects while
leaving their different standards visible.

## Keep three kinds of statement apart


**Documented world:** a source supports a stated event, rule or institutional
claim at a particular date. A source may report a party's assertion rather than
establish that assertion. A court order, an agency filing and a scientific
reconstruction also perform different evidential jobs.

**Interpretation:** the author selects a boundary, connects evidence and offers
an explanation. Readers can challenge that selection, especially where it
implies motives, causation, inevitability or a missing voice.

**Constructed situation or proposed correction:** a possible arrangement makes
the question concrete. It has not happened merely because it is feasible in a
story. A proposal needs an owner with legitimate authority, resources and a
way to find out whether it worked.

Each finished case must retain at least one correction to its initial reading.
That correction is part of the argument: it shows where a plausible first
account gave too much credit to an intention, document, resource or analogy.
It is not a score awarded to the framework.

## How to disagree usefully

Read a case with a different possible account in mind. Which entity falls
outside the author's boundary? Which evidence comes from one shared source?
Could the proposed protection arrive too late, or make another person's route
worse? Does a stronger existing method already answer the question more clearly?
If no practical change follows, that is a useful result to report.

You can read without adopting the terminology. These are worked inquiries,
not a requirement to fill every field before acting. Urgent action, specialist
advice and ordinary human care must not be displaced by casebook completion.
