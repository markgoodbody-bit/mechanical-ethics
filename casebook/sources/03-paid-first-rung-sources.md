## Review and correction

An internal Build source reviewer, separately identified from CC and Codex
Windows, checked the funding scope and causal cautions. It confirmed three
necessary boundaries: qualifying training mentorship is conditional; funding
rules do not govern every ordinary starter job; policy targets and hiring data
do not establish delivery or AI causation. These boundaries appear explicitly
in the case. This was an AI source challenge, not peer review.

The initial-reading correction is retained in the chapter: money labelled
training plus a feedback form does not establish an affordable supported role.
No qualifying mentoring was categorically declared unfundable, and no extra
ME reporting duty was introduced.
