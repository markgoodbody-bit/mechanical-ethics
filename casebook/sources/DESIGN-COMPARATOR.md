# Constructive-design comparison: first editorial pass

Framework Build, 26 October 2026. This is a bounded comparison of presentation
and evidence, not a ranking of casebooks or an effectiveness study.

## Primary example inspected

[Public Design Evidence Review: Case Study Bank](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-public-design-evidence-review/public-design-evidence-review-case-study-bank-html),
Cabinet Office / Policy Profession, updated 9 March 2026. Read the introduction
and complete HMRC plastic-packaging-tax case in the returned HTML, including its
problem, design work and reported outcomes. The other twelve cases and linked
documents were not reviewed in this pass.

The introduction identifies a selected collection rather than comprehensive
coverage. The HMRC example connects a practical design question to supply-chain
work, administrative constraints and subsequent choices. It distinguishes a
reported change in demand from a causal claim about the tax. These are useful
editorial practices: expose the work between intention and decision, state the
selection boundary, and separate an outcome from attribution. The account is
an institutional contribution, not an independent evaluation of its own design
methods. No figures or substantial source wording are reproduced here.

A London City Hall community-design story appeared in search, but direct opening
returned HTTP 403. It was not bypassed or counted as a read comparator. Search
snippets were not promoted to a complete case review.

## Gap in our first edition

Our chapters provide substantial interpretation before readers have much chance
to form their own account. This is Build's editorial diagnosis, not a finding
from the comparator's authors. Source precision alone does not establish a good
learning experience.

## Concrete change

Added optional [reader decision exercises](../05-try-another-account.md) that
invite an initial judgement, a changed assumption and a counter-reading. They
retain the distinction between constructed prompts and historical evidence.
They neither supply operational instructions nor claim tested pedagogy. No
additional case is added merely to increase the count.

Cross-comparisons with law, engineering incident analysis and public administration
are separately assigned to CC and Codex. Those assignments are not completed
reviews until their actual returns are read and integrated.
