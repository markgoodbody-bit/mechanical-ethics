# Mechanical Ethics v1.0.0 — released edition

**Current formal released baseline · 26 October 2026 · independent criticism invited**

Mechanical Ethics asks how people can make a better future when knowledge is
partial, powers differ and correction may arrive too late. It proposes a
portable, middle-out reasoning language: begin with an actual situation, follow
what can change and who can change it, and check whether help becomes effective.
Its questions concern care, agency, evidence, responsibility, repair and the
worthwhile possibilities people can build together.

that repair does not restore, shared capability and distributed responsibility.

The language cannot derive moral standing or legitimate authority from a
description. It does not replace medicine, law, safety engineering or other
specialist methods. Practical efficacy, independent novice comprehension,
accessibility, disputed values and real-world timing remain open. The examples
are explanatory constructions except for the attributed historical cases;
they are not results of field trials. No peer review or outside adoption is claimed.

## Help test it

An independent reader's disagreement is useful. You do not need to accept the
framework to identify where it fails. For example:

- Where did you first lose the thread, and what did you think the passage meant?
- Does a distinction clarify a real decision, or merely rename it?
- Can you find a counterexample, missing affected party or hidden assumption?
- Does it make protection or a constructive next step more usable in practice?
- Where does another discipline already supply a stronger method?

[Open a review issue](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/mechanical-ethics/issues/new)
with the chapter or passage, your reading, and the correction or counterexample
you propose. Questions about collaboration, stewardship or continuing the work
can use the same route. The repository owner is Mark; an issue is a route to
request consideration, not a promise of response time or acceptance.

Issues are public: please omit private testimony, identifying case details,
credentials and confidential documents. You need not disclose a personal story
to challenge the argument. This is not an urgent-help or monitored complaints channel.
