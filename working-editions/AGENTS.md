# Editing the reader editions

Use this check on the assembled manuscript after integrating chapter, source or review edits.

- Read each chapter's opening, its main example and its ending in order. Name one thing the reader can now see, check, do or build. If it is hard to name, put that result into the scene or the explanation before adding any further qualification.
- Keep a qualification beside the result when it changes the evidence needed, the possible action, consent, authority, safety or uncertainty. State a condition as part of the decision it affects. Put general scope and evidence status in the Notes, and put build history in the draft record.
- Check the assembled reading copy as well as the source. Scan for repeated descriptions of the book's limits and for passages that end at a warning. Read every candidate in context. The test is whether the reader can tell what to do with the account and what evidence would support their next move.
- Walk one repair and one formation example through the edited text. Trace who does the work, which conditions support it and what the reader could check afterwards. Follow the existing citations and verify that the edit still makes the relationship to each source clear.
