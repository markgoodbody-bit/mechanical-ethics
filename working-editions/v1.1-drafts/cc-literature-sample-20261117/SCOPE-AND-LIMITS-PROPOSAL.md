Task: [COM #955](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/955), item 3. Draft for Campfire's integration and Mark's decision. Line numbers refer to the released v1.0 manuscript (SHA-256 `1315a2c2…37a8`).

## The rule used

Move a qualification to this section when it is **generic**: it would be true of any passage and repeats a condition the reader already has. Keep it in place when removing it would **change what the sentence claims**, or would lead a reader to act wrongly at that point. That follows the brief: "Keep a condition near a claim when removing it would change that claim's meaning or lead the reader to act wrongly."

## Proposed section (for the Notes; it replaces the opening note's limits paragraph and absorbs the repeats listed below)

> ## Scope and limits
>
> These conditions apply to every chapter. They are stated once here so that the chapters can say what they mean.
>
> **What a description can settle.** The language describes what changed, who could act, which routes were usable, what was repaired and what remains. It does not assign rights, decide moral standing, set priorities between competing needs, or make a remedy happen. Those need the people affected, declared values, evidence and legitimate authority. Where a chapter asks "who can decide", it means whoever actually holds that authority under the law and institutions involved.
>
> **Specialist methods govern their own ground.** Medicine, law, safety engineering, scheduling, ecology and other fields have stronger methods and duties. This book's questions help locate where those methods are needed and carry evidence to them. They do not replace them.
>
> **Ordered and complex situations.** Routes, clocks and the stages of correction work where cause and effect can be traced. Where an outcome comes from many parts responding to each other, map the constraints and act in small, watched steps instead of tracing chains or estimating causal shares (Chapter 12).
>
> **Arrangements, not motives.** The book describes barriers, incentives, delays and who benefits from them. Unless separate evidence shows deliberate obstruction, those descriptions do not establish anyone's intentions. The predator position (Chapter 9) is defined by what an arrangement does, not by what someone wants.
>
> **Uncertainty.** UNKNOWN is a result, not an instruction. On its own it neither orders an activity to continue nor orders it to stop. It does create work for whoever can reduce the uncertainty and has a duty to (Chapter 6).
>
> **Evidence for this book.** The household, library and workshop scenes are constructed for explanation. The Kelsey and *Challenger* examples come from the official records cited in the Notes. No field trial, peer review or independent test of whether new readers understand it has been established for this edition. Agreement among the AI systems that helped draft it is not independent confirmation.

The safety note ("If you are being harmed now", line 20) stays at the front. It is an instruction to a reader who may be at risk, not a qualification.
