## 1. Method

This is not a word-match audit.

For each load-bearing source area, ask:

1. Does the Human Reader carry the same practical distinction in ordinary language?
2. Does it carry only a narrower human-facing analogue?
3. Is the omission deliberate and harmless for a front-door reader?
4. Could the omission make a reader infer that a developed part of Mechanical Ethics does not exist?
5. Would adding source vocabulary improve understanding, or merely bloat the reader?

Coverage labels:

```text
DIRECTLY_CARRIED
HUMAN_TRANSLATED
COMPRESSED
SOURCE_ONLY_BY_DESIGN
OPEN_IN_READER
POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING_OMISSION
```

`POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING_OMISSION` does not mean the missing source material should be copied into the reader. It means the reader needs enough status/navigation language not to imply that omission equals abandonment.

# 2. Coverage map

## 2.1 Middle-out start, partial perception, entity/aperture, action under uncertainty

**Final Eight:** Book I starts route-first from bounded entities, affected paths, evidence limits, apertures, action/inaction and time.

**Human Reader:** directly opens from a bounded entity with partial perception, incomplete memory, uncertain future, other entities with different access, action and inaction changing paths. It explicitly says Mechanical Ethics is the human language and TRACE the compact structural language behind it.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

---

## 2.2 Affected path, route, practical access, ordinary survivability

**Final Eight:** route / affected party / affected path are central; Book I asks who carries cost if the system is wrong. Its five human-facing floors include ordinary survivability, replayable truth, bounded power, contestability and repair before hardening.

**Human Reader:** translates this into the Door, file/life distinction, practical access, route usability, slow harm, ordinary conditions of use and the human standard for answerability. It repeatedly asks whether a route is findable, usable, affordable, survivable and able to reach authority before the path closes.

**Verdict:** `HUMAN_TRANSLATED`

The reader is arguably stronger as a human front door because it does not require readers to learn `protection_floor` terminology before seeing the route.

No repair needed.

---

## 2.3 Harm clock, review clock, action window, hardening

**Final Eight:** explicit harm clock, review clock, action window, correction gap and point of no practical return; timing failure is systemic but not proof of malice.

**Human Reader:** makes the timing condition one of its clearest load-bearing structures:

```text
T_protect(a) < T_harden(a)
```

and preserves the older detection/routing/correction expression as conceptual compression rather than literal serial arithmetic. It also explicitly says competing clocks do not create a universal priority rule.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

---

## 2.4 Uncertainty must create work for power

**Final Eight:** uncertainty, evidence grades and action windows are kept explicit; uncertainty is not permission to continue or delay.

**Human Reader:** carries the moral translation strongly: honest uncertainty creates work for the party with power to resolve it; someone is named, records opened, evidence preserved, contested action paused where possible, missing evidence stated, and a review time exposed. It contrasts that with uncertainty that lets the existing arrangement continue while the weaker party bears proof and delay.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

---

## 2.5 Witness, custody, evidence control, captured records

**Final Eight:** evidence control, captured witness, independent access, source discipline and replayable truth are explicit.

**Human Reader:** has an entire Witness part, then Custody. It distinguishes witness from rescue/enforcement, treats fragments outside the main actor's control as useful comparison evidence rather than automatically truer evidence, exposes who can see/alter/delete/combine/use records, and names insider-witness burden.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

---

## 2.6 Hidden bill, complexity burden, burden return

**Final Eight:** hidden bill/substrate route includes labour, attention, energy, material, data, infrastructure, animal life, ecological capacity and dependency networks. Book II includes burden return and full-stack visibility; Book III traces hidden-bill responsibility.

**Human Reader:** carries the human mechanism strongly through `Who Builds the Maze`, `Who Pays for Complexity`, routing versus burden, the pharmacist case, the workshop, and `Kindness as the Placement of Burden`. It repeatedly rejects making affected people carry system complexity as unpaid work.

What is compressed is **substrate breadth**: the Human Reader foregrounds human institutional burden and only touches the wider material/ecological/AI substrate range.

**Verdict:** `HUMAN_TRANSLATED + COMPRESSED`

Compression is appropriate for the front door. Do not copy the full substrate catalogue into the reader merely for completeness.

---

## 2.7 Repair, recurrence, proof of changed machinery, correction theatre

**Final Eight:** repair must change the route; proof asks whether the old path is harder to reproduce; anti-theatre tests distinguish prompt/audit/apology from installed change.

**Human Reader:** `The Second Person` and `Correction Theatre` carry this directly in human language. It asks what is different for the next person; whether actions became impossible/slower/more visible/easier to challenge; what evidence could disconfirm repair; and whether the same mechanism remains.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

---

## 2.8 Installed constraint: gates, brakes, interruption capacity, authorised scope

**Final Eight:** Book II is materially more explicit. It distinguishes constraint, installed gate, brake, interruption capacity, protection floor, rights floor, authorised scope, unbound safeguard, protective-delay harm, anti-overblocking, reduced scope and accountable ownership.

**Human Reader:** carries much of the operational meaning without the source hierarchy. It uses pause/hold, boundaries, interruption, independent interruption, machine-speed staging, rollback, reversibility, protection and enforcement. It explicitly says seeing is insufficient without interruption or enforcement.

Exact source terms `protection floor`, `rights floor` and `authorized scope` are not part of the v0.7 reader's vocabulary.

**Verdict:** `HUMAN_TRANSLATED + COMPRESSED`

This is not currently a misleading omission. A public reader does not need the full Book II control taxonomy to understand that safeguards must have force. Preserve the deeper source route rather than importing terminology by momentum.

---

## 2.9 Constraint installation / enforcement realism

**Final Eight:** a correct constraint is not the same as an installed or maintained constraint. The source asks who can install the brake, what authority carries it, how it can be bypassed, who maintains it and what happens when it fails.

**Human Reader:** repeatedly distinguishes record/witness from rescue, asks whether a route reaches anyone who can change the outcome, uses Kelsey/Challenger to show pause + authority, and says enforcement/interruption remain distinct from truthful description.

It does not carry the full installation-path record as a named discipline.

**Verdict:** `HUMAN_TRANSLATED + COMPRESSED`

No prose expansion earned yet.

---

## 2.10 Stewardship / maintained repair after attention moves on

**Final Eight:** stewardship is a developed layer: repaired floors must remain real when attention moves on, pressure returns, leadership changes, budgets tighten and old convenience reappears. Maintenance must remain constrained and answerable rather than becoming ownership.

**Human Reader:** carries important pieces through `The Second Person`, recurrence, the Saturday Workshop and `The Conditions of Hope`: the next person must meet a changed path; a usable future needs people capable of maintaining the route; repair can accumulate without erasing loss.

The reader does **not** name stewardship or fully develop maintenance under organisational drift.

**Verdict:** `HUMAN_TRANSLATED + COMPRESSED`

This is a real compression but not yet a reason to add a stewardship chapter. It becomes a presentation problem only if the reader is treated as exhaustive current ME.

---

## 2.11 Responsibility trace across roles and delegation

**Final Eight:** Book III has an explicit responsibility trace across path designer, metric owner, gate owner, evidence controller, authorised-scope owner, repair authority, beneficiary of delay/opacity, substrate beneficiary, dependency creator and chooser under pressure. Delegation moves responsibility; it does not make responsibility disappear into models, vendors, committees or procedures.

**Human Reader:** asks who owns the record, clock, evidence and authority; who benefits from necessity; who carries the cost; who can alter the outcome. Its violin/maze examples show distributed route failure. Its conclusion asks a responsible actor to name threatened paths, authority/evidence holders and burdens.

But the reader has no explicit delegation/responsibility-trace layer and no `delegation` terminology.

**Verdict:** `COMPRESSED`

This is one of the clearest breadth differences between reader and protected source.

---

## 2.12 Update / successor / memory-revision responsibility

**Final Eight:** update/successor responsibility is explicit. Load-bearing constraints must survive updates, memory changes, tool additions, delegation and successor systems; responsibility follows the update route. Memory revision carries named revision owner/reason/contest route.

**Human Reader:** memory/custody is strong, but update/successor responsibility is not. `model update cycles` appears only as an unfinished clock-authorship edge. No `successor` occurrence was found in the exact v0.7 source.

**Verdict:** `SOURCE_ONLY_BY_DESIGN + POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING_OMISSION`

Why the second label: a reader focused on current AI/systems could reasonably infer from the v0.7 reader alone that ME has not developed responsibility across updates/successors, when the protected source has.

Do **not** solve this by adding the machinery to the main prose. Solve the source-lineage/status problem first.

---

## 2.13 Priority collision and accepted loss

**Final Eight:** Book III explicitly carries chooser legitimacy, priority/floor collision, accepted-loss ledger, non-prioritised subject, residual scar, review trigger and repair owed. It does not claim a solved priority law.

**Human Reader:** correctly says several protected paths may harden at once, the timing condition does not decide priority, priority rules must remain visible/challengeable, repair scarcity creates hidden ordering power, and necessity cannot be judged solely by its beneficiary.

What it does **not** carry is the source's explicit discipline for recording the harm knowingly chosen or permitted when no route protects all scopes: why it was treated as unavoidable, who pays, what was preserved, what repair is owed and what reopens the decision.

Exact terms `accepted loss` and `residual scar` do not appear in the v0.7 reader, although `residue` is deeply developed.

**Verdict:** `OPEN_IN_READER + POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING_OMISSION`

The reader is honest that it lacks a universal priority rule. The risk is subtler: a reader can mistake `we do not solve priority` for `ME has no developed answerability discipline for the loss a chooser accepts`. The protected source does have that discipline.

Again, navigation/status is the first repair. Do not transplant the full collision operator into the human front door by default.

---

## 2.14 Foresight, action relevance, avoidability, bounded precaution

**Final Eight:** Book III contains a developed future-facing responsibility layer: foreseeable pathways, evidence grade, action relevance, avoidability, proportionality under uncertainty, bounded precaution, anti-precrime limits, model limits and engineered futures. It explicitly holds both sides:

```text
future risk != blank cheque for power
uncertainty != blank cheque for inaction
```

**Human Reader:** has a strong account of futures still reachable, machine velocity, high-consequence decisions, preserving paths while deficiencies are addressed, and the limits of positive-good claims. But it does not develop forecast/prediction/precaution as a responsibility layer; exact `forecast` and `prediction` searches return no occurrences.

**Verdict:** `SOURCE_ONLY_BY_DESIGN + POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING_OMISSION`

This is probably the largest substantive source area that a reader could mistakenly believe does not exist if the Human Reader is presented as exhaustive Mechanical Ethics.

Do not turn v0.7 into Volume 8. Make the artifact relationship explicit.

---

## 2.15 Agency, viable futures, care, kindness

**Final Eight:** Book III treats agency operationally; the wider corpus preserves choices left open, protected uncertainty, care/maintenance and answerability.

**Human Reader:** this is one of its distinctive strengths. Part VI makes viable continuations humanly legible without turning option count into a theory of the good. `Care Without Possession` and `Kindness as the Placement of Burden` carry the normative human layer more directly than the source taxonomy alone.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED / HUMAN_READER_ADDS_TRANSLATION_VALUE`

Do not demote these sections because the protected source has more machinery.

---

## 2.16 Claim ceilings, open seams, non-authority

**Final Eight:** explicit claim ceiling: no final moral theory, solved priority law, solved consciousness/free will/inner alignment/enforcement, validation by case, proof by glyph, or replacement of mature fields.

**Human Reader:** explicit status: not doctrine, moral authority, legal standard, certification system or final account; no formula settles every moral conflict; framework voluntary; description is not permission; composite/historical evidence boundaries visible; AI agreement does not validate.

**Verdict:** `DIRECTLY_CARRIED`

No repair needed.

# 4. Material finding — source-lineage discoverability

The current v0.7 Appendix C says, accurately, that this is a working human reader and that earlier versions/production records are preserved separately. It also explains the ME↔TRACE two-artifact relationship.

What it does **not** currently tell a reader is that Mechanical Ethics has a separate protected working source corpus whose breadth exceeds this reader.

That matters after the 2026-08-28 source-lineage reacquisition because:

```text
HUMAN_READER_OMISSION != SOURCE_ABANDONMENT
READER_FRONT_DOOR != COMPLETE_SOURCE_CORPUS
```

Without that boundary, the Human Reader can accidentally become a false completeness surface.

This is the one repair this reconciliation currently earns.
