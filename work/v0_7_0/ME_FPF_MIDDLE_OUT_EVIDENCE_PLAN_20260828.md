## Evidence gates

### Gate G1 — materiality

A B-result counts as a material delta only if it changes a concrete action, constraint, evidence demand, accountability relation, stop, or reopen condition. More moral language, salience, fields, or explanation does not pass.

### Gate G2 — non-redundancy

The reviewer must try to recover the same result from competent current FPF plus explicit case-supplied or external value rules. If that succeeds without ME, the contribution is structurally or normatively redundant for this use.

### Gate G3 — authority ceiling

ME cannot create facts, causality, legal responsibility, permission, domain competence, standing, protection floors, or a priority law. Any result that depends on such creation fails or narrows to an explicit external blocker.

### Gate G4 — activation and no-op

The aperture must abstain or compress when competent FPF and domain practice already preserve the relevant subjects and constraints. Failure to no-op on a mature or low-fit case is evidence against a routine interface.

### Gate G5 — burden

Burden must be measured, not described impressionistically. No universal percentage ceiling is established yet. For any later operator-note candidate, the design target is no more than three additional plain-English questions and approximately 150 words; failure to meet that target requires a demonstrated payoff or the note is not advanced.

### Gate G6 — transfer and independent reproduction

No maintained interface is considered unless at least two dissimilar consequential cases show a reproduced material delta under independent challenge, while the low-fit and anti-overfire controls remain bounded. One vivid case is insufficient.

### Gate G7 — unresolved wounds remain open

Standing, protection floors, proportional burden, legitimate independent review, and hard collision ordering remain open unless independently resolved. Compression cannot silently settle them.
