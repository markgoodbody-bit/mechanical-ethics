## Question under attack

> Can Mechanical Ethics function as an FPF-compatible normative layer/profile without becoming redundant, sovereign, pseudo-doctrinal, operationally unusable, or distorted by the host framework?

This audit deliberately distinguishes a weak compatibility claim from a strong product/conformance claim:

```text
H_LOOSE := ME can operate as a contestable external/optional normative companion over an FPF-structured situation.
H_NATIVE := ME is already justified as an FPF-native plug-in / DPF / LPF or distributable integrated product.
```

The audit tries to kill both. A result that preserves `H_LOOSE` while breaking `H_NATIVE` is an adverse/narrowing result, not a success by default.

## What survives as plausible ME remainder

The audit gives no novelty credit for structural objects FPF can already recover. The plausible remainder is normative pressure placed on those objects, especially:

1. affected beings should remain consequentially present in decisions acting on their lives;
2. unresolved uncertainty should not be cheaply exported to parties with less power when actors with control can carry the work;
3. complexity / proof / waiting / repair burden should be examined for transfer toward source, controller or beneficiary rather than defaulting to the edge;
4. a beneficiary of delay, necessity or closure should not be the sole judge of it;
5. nominal correction must not erase residue or accepted loss;
6. technically open continuations can be normatively inadmissible as 'usable' when coercive, seriously unsafe, unaffordable, unchallengeable or too late;
7. dirty collisions must keep the non-prioritised subject and accepted loss visible;
8. ME's refusal to supply a universal priority law is itself an integration boundary: any additional priority rule / legitimate selector must be named.

These claims are not proven correct by compatibility. Some are explicit normative posits whose justification remains contestable.

## Strongest adverse findings

### 1. Structural redundancy is extensive
Affected-scope discovery, option/route structure, timing/currentness, evidence/reliance boundaries, reversibility, and result-vs-decision/work separation do not earn ME distinctness merely because ME gives them morally salient names.

### 2. ME underdetermines hard collisions by design
Prior pump-case work demonstrated that ME can change burden/route interpretation while still requiring an **additional priority rule** to choose between protected paths. That is compatible with `H_LOOSE` but blocks a selector-shaped plug-in.

### 3. Several ME norms import contestable commitments
Prior hostile work already identified under-grounded commitments around power asymmetry, proportionality, comparable repair, a thin conception of meaningful life, and care/kindness language. FPF compatibility does not derive or validate those commitments.

### 4. Full-corpus integration is operationally implausible
Final Eight is a protected, heterogeneous historical/source corpus, not an appropriate routine FPF payload. A compact interface would be needed, but compacting it risks laundering open wounds into pseudo-doctrine.

### 5. FPF is a moving dependency
Any interop mapping must bind an exact FPF edition and have a refresh/reacquisition rule. Static semantic crosswalks will stale.

### 6. Licensing is unresolved
No compatible public distribution/reuse basis was established in this audit. Do not ship an integrated plug-in or copy FPF material into ME under an assumed licence. This remains a human/legal gate.

### Drift interpretation

The project has **not** drifted merely by comparing ME with FPF. The comparison is aligned with the purpose because it tests whether existing external machinery can carry structure more cheaply and whether ME has a genuine normative role.

The dangerous drift would be to:
- recast ME into FPF terminology merely to gain legitimacy;
- treat FPF's 'admissible action' language as borrowed moral authority for ME;
- delete the Human Reader or Final Eight because an interop card is more machine-friendly;
- preserve ME as a separate framework only because project identity makes retirement emotionally difficult;
- rebuild TRACE as an unnecessary bridge between FPF and ME after the current evidence has reduced that need.

## Next falsifiable boundary

Only one next object is earned, and even it should begin as an experiment rather than product architecture:

```text
same frozen consequential case
A := competent FPF-only reading
B := same FPF reading + bounded ME normative aperture

measure:
  consequential omission / recovery
  changed constraint or decision rationale
  explicit external value rule
  accepted-loss / residue visibility
  overfire / paternalism / unsupported norm
  total input + output + operator burden
  no-op correctness on low-fit control
```

A positive result requires a repeatable, decision-relevant or accountability-relevant delta at acceptable burden. More moral language, more fields, or a different answer is not enough.

A null/adverse result is admissible and could support a thinner role, interoperability-only guidance, or preserving ME principally as a human philosophical instrument rather than an FPF-facing maintained product.
