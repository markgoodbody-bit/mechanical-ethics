## Figure boundaries

### Figure 1 — The file and the life

Shows a lived situation passing through a narrow portal into a factual but incomplete institutional record. It does not establish that the life account is complete or always superior.

### Figure 2 — The correction window

Shows protection before a practical hardening boundary and later correction after it. The boundary is schematic, not measured, sharp, universal, or supplied by the figure.

### Figure 3 — Two flats, one wall

Shows the composite scene's shared material source and different routes, clocks, authority, and retained futures. It remains illustrative and does not become empirical evidence because it has a diagram.

### Figure 4 — Machine speed versus human correction

Shows repeated automated commitments outrunning a slower correction route and a staged path with a brake. It does not assert a universal machine/human speed ratio or that a represented brake is independent, tested, authorised, or effective.
