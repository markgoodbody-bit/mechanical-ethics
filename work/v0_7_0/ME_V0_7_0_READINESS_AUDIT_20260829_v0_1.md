## Question

Is the current reader sufficiently controlled and candid for bounded criticism by an external neighbouring project, and what still prevents stronger claims?

## 1. Earned results

- Candidate v0.2 is a coherent, human-facing reader with exact recorded identity.
- The central timing condition is stated in prose and notation and is explicitly not a universal priority rule.
- The reader separates formal routes from usable routes, file correction from lived repair, evidence from custody, and case repair from mechanism change.
- Composite scenes and explanatory figures are explicitly non-evidential.
- The reader admits the absence of enforcement and preserves standing, collision, floor and scarcity problems as open.
- Warm model reviews caused only narrow wording and production changes; relay agreement was not treated as validation.

## 2. Provisional ideas

- The thin condition of remaining present in decisions about one's life may be a useful normative floor, but its grounding and scope are incomplete.
- Burden should usually move toward actors with greater power and control, but no complete allocation rule exists.
- A loose, optional relationship with FPF may be useful; an FPF-native plugin, conformance claim or compulsory sequence is not earned.

## 3. Wording pressures still worth challenging

- “Unknown remains honest only while someone carries a real duty to reduce it” is rhetorically clear but broader than the surrounding institutional case. It may need a power-, consequence- or feasibility-bound qualification.
- “A door deserves moral credit only to the extent that it opens” is understandable but compresses partial accessibility, competing duties and comparative improvement into one line.
- The high-consequence checklist in the Conclusion may impose unrealistic analysis burden if read as applying equally to ordinary subjects and large institutions.
- “The affected being” works for many cases but does not itself solve representation for children, animals, impaired adults, groups or disputed artificial subjects.

These are live falsification targets, not automatic edits. Narrowing them without a concrete counterexample could hide rather than solve the underlying foundation problem.

## 4. Governance and process improvements

- Keep v0.6.3 frozen until a separate promotion decision.
- Keep candidate v0.2 and the withdrawn v0.3 wording experiment as distinct objects.
- Verify candidate bytes, figures and critical misuse ceiling mechanically.
- Keep the human-reader test result separate from model review.
- Present FPF work as external challenge and overlap testing, not as validation or inherited authority.

## 5. Unresolved problems

- standing under protected-scope uncertainty;
- hard collisions and competing clocks;
- grounding and ordering of protection floors;
- representation and decision authority where the subject cannot answer directly;
- necessity or closure where the beneficiary is also judge;
- residue triage when repair capacity is finite;
- stable harm regimes in which truth is known but accepted by power;
- usability for ordinary people without unusual time, confidence, money, safety or institutional reserves.
