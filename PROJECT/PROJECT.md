# Mechanical Ethics — project

## Purpose

Mechanical Ethics is the human-facing artifact in a two-artifact project. It examines what happens when limited actors and institutions make consequential choices under uncertainty, especially when correction arrives after a threatened path has hardened.

TRACE is the separable structure-facing artifact. Neither validates the other.

## What the current candidate earned

- The timing condition is separated from a universal priority rule.
- Common clock basis and overlapping rather than automatically serial correction work are explicit.
- Formal route availability is separated from practical usability and meaningful exit.
- Evidence state, custody, staleness, measurement effects, mechanism change and residue are expressed in human prose.
- Four explanatory figures are present as editable SVG and PNG carriers and are explicitly non-evidential.
- External-model review earned only narrow wording and production repairs; agreement was not treated as validation.

## What remains unresolved

- standing under protected-scope uncertainty;
- allocation in hard collisions and competing clocks;
- grounding and ordering of protection floors;
- necessity and closure where the beneficiary is also judge;
- residue triage under finite capacity;
- domain-specific interruption and enforcement authority;
- whether ordinary readers and affected people can use the account without unusual time, confidence or reserves.
